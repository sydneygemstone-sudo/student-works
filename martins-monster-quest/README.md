# 🎮 Martin's Monster Quest — Full 2D Adventure

> A complete 2D monster-catching RPG adventure designed for Martin.
> Includes character selection, starter choice, story prologue, follower companion, full overworld exploration, and turn-based battles.

---

## ⚡ Quick Launch

### Option 1: Direct Open in Browser
Double-click `index.html` or run in terminal:
```bash
open /Users/gemstone/dev/martins-monster-quest/index.html
```

### Option 2: Local Web Server (For iPad on same Wi-Fi)
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory /Users/gemstone/dev/martins-monster-quest
```
Then open `http://localhost:8080` (or `http://<your-mac-ip>:8080` on iPad).

## 🌟 Adventure Features

1. **Trainer Selection (人物选择):**
   * **Martin:** Red cap & blue trainer jacket (Spirited & brave).
   * **Sky:** Green bandana & forest scout jacket (Nature tracker).
   * **Leo:** Gold goggles & purple hoodie (Tech researcher).

2. **Starter Creature Selection (初始神兽选择):**
   * **Flameling 🔥 (Fire Type):** High attack cub. Special Move: *Flame Spark*.
   * **Leafbit 🌿 (Nature Type):** High defense bunny. Special Move: *Leaf Slice*.
   * **Aquapup 💧 (Water Type):** Balanced swift pup. Special Move: *Water Pulse*.

3. **Story Prologue (完整冒险故事序幕):**
   * Professor Elm warns you about the thunderstorm gathering atop **Stormjaw's Peak** in the north.
   * You receive your partner creature and Monster Orbs to set out on your journey!

4. **Follower Companion (萌宠跟随系统):**
   * Just like in classic RPGs (Pokémon Yellow/HeartGold), your chosen starter walks directly behind your character on the map!
   * Turn around and press **[A]** to pet and interact with your companion!

5. **Living Overworld Map (生动完整大世界地图):**
   * **Trainer Lab & Cottage:** Starting home with red tile roof and windows.
   * **Cobblestone Paths & Wild Flowers:** Yellow, red, and purple blossoms.
   * **Whispering Meadow (Tall Grass):** Animated swaying grass patches.
   * **Roaming Wild Creatures:** Visible wild Leafbits & Aquapups wandering in the grass!
   * **Healing Spring:** Crystal-blue pool with animated heart sparkles; step in for full party HP restoration and chime!
   * **Stormjaw's Mountain Dais:** Northern stone gorge leading to the legendary Boss surrounded by lightning bolts.

6. **Pre-Battle Encounter Transition (战斗切入动画):**
   * Exclamation mark ("!") bubbles up over your head with an alert chirp!
   * Dramatic screen flash & battle transition before entering the turn-based arena.

7. **Tactical Battles & Level Progression:**
   * Dynamic attack button with current move name (*Flame Spark*, *Ember Blast*, etc.).
   * Capture animation with 3 wobbles; weakened wild creatures join your party.
   * **⭐ LEVEL UP!** fanfare banner with stat boosts (+8 Max HP, +3 Attack) and full heal bonus.
   * Final Boss Showdown with **Stormjaw ⚡** leading to the **🏆 MARTIN WINS!** victory screen.

## 🕹️ Controls

* **Keyboard (Mac / PC):**
  * **Move:** `W A S D` or `Arrow Keys`
  * **Interact / Attack / Confirm [A]:** `Space` or `Enter` or `Z`
  * **Run / Back [B]:** `Escape` or `R`
  * **Quick Battle Keys:** `1` (Attack), `2` (Capture), `3` (Switch), `4` (Run)
* **iPad / Touch Screen:**
  * **Touch D-Pad:** Bottom-left directional buttons.
  * **Tap to Move:** Tap directly anywhere on the canvas map to walk towards it.
  * **Virtual Buttons:** Green `[A]` for Action/Attack, Red `[B]` for Run.
